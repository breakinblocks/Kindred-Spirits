The charm is your link to your spirit. It carries no data of its own: the bond
is stored on you, so every charm you own opens the same companion and losing
one loses nothing.

## Two ways to use it

- **Sneak and right-click a tamed spirit** to bond with it. Do the same on your
  bonded spirit to release the bond. If you are already bonded to something else
  it refuses and tells you so.
- **Right-click on its own** to open the bond screen.

The "Open Kindred Charm" keybind opens the bond screen too, as long as a charm
is somewhere in your inventory. It has no key by default; set one under
Controls. With Curios installed the charm also fits your necklace slot, and the
keybind works from there.

## The bond screen

The screen shows your spirit on the left with its name, level, stars, health,
attack, armour and bond, and a column of buttons on the right. Click and drag
the portrait to turn your spirit around; double-click it to reset the view.

- **Summon** brings a resting spirit out beside you. **Call to me** fetches one
  that is out in the world, wherever it is. If the charm cannot find it, the
  screen says "Out of reach" and Call to me re-forms it beside you from what the
  charm remembers. **Send to rest** tucks it away in
  the charm, where it is safe from harm and still keeps everything it has earned.
- **Orders** cycles Follow, Stay and Wander. Stay sits it down where it is.
  Wander lets it roam. The "Cycle Companion Orders" keybind does the same from
  outside the screen, as does sneaking and right-clicking the spirit with an
  empty hand.
- **Aggression** cycles Passive, Neutral and Aggressive. Passive never fights.
  Neutral fights back and defends you. Aggressive also joins your fights and
  hunts monsters on sight.
- **Release bond** lets the spirit go. It stays tamed but stops growing.
- **Prestige** appears at level 30, see [Stars and Prestige](./stars).
- An **ability button** appears under the portrait when the spirit has an
  ability you can trigger yourself, with its cooldown in the label.
- The **gear row** shows what it is wearing, with a small button to take it off.
  See [Equipment](./equipment).

## Stats and Abilities

Two buttons at the bottom of the button column open a popup over the screen.
Close it with the x in its corner or with Escape.

- **Stats** lists every attribute your spirit has right now with its level 1
  base in brackets. A green number means something is adding to it: levels,
  stars, an ability passive or a piece of gear. Below that are the bond-derived
  numbers: how much faster it earns experience, how many storage slots you can
  reach, and how long a revive takes.
- **Abilities** lists every ability of the species with what it needs to
  unlock, and an **On / Off** switch for each one you have. Scroll with the
  mouse wheel if the list runs long. See [Abilities](./abilities) for what
  switching one off does.

Both work while the spirit is resting.

Type in the name box to rename it; leave it empty to go back to the species
name. A Mini Player also gets a skin box, where a player name puts that player's
skin on it and an empty box means your own.

Orders, aggression, name and skin all work while the spirit is resting, so you
can set it up before you summon it.

## When your spirit dies

A bonded spirit that dies is not lost. The charm goes into recovery for five
minutes (less with a high bond, down to two and a half minutes at bond 30), and
then **Summon** brings it back. It comes back at the same level, but loses half
of its progress towards the next one and one bond level if its bond was above
10.

While the charm recovers you can still open the screen, rename the spirit, and
release the bond if you want to move on.

<Callout variant="warning">
    Only a bonded spirit gets a second chance. A tamed spirit with no bond dies
    for good.
</Callout>

## Healing

Right-click a hurt spirit with a golden apple to heal it by 10 health. At full
health the apple is eaten as an ordinary meal instead.

A **Spirit Bandage** is the cheap option: two paper and a string make two, and
each one restores 6 health (3 hearts) to a hurt spirit you own.
