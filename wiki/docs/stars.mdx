At level 30 a spirit has nowhere left to grow, so it can start again with a
star. A spirit can earn up to **five stars**, shown next to its level on the
bond screen and in its name plate.

## Prestiging

Open the bond screen at level 30 and press **Prestige**. Your spirit goes back to
level 1 with no experience, gains one star, and keeps everything else: bond,
name, skin, orders, aggression and equipment. The button disappears once it has
five stars.

## What a star gives

Every star raises stat growth by **10%**, so a five star spirit gains half again
as much per level as a fresh one and ends far stronger at level 30.

Each star also grants a permanent bonus that only comes from stars:

- **1 star**: +10% max health
- **2 stars**: +0.1 knockback resistance
- **3 stars**: +5% movement speed
- **4 stars**: +2 armour
- **5 stars**: +10% attack damage, and a gold name plate

<Callout variant="info">
    Bond is untouched by prestige, so keep the bond feeds going while you level
    back up; a high bond makes the next climb faster.
</Callout>
