Bond is how close you and your spirit are. It runs from level 0 to 30 and is the
slow, steady part of raising a companion: it comes from time spent together and
cannot be farmed.

## Earning bond

Your spirit earns **one bond point every second** it is out in the world with
you, in the same dimension and within 32 blocks. Each bond level costs more than
the last, starting at 760 points and rising 6% per level, so reaching bond 30
takes about 50 Minecraft days of travelling together.

Nothing is earned while you are away from the keyboard: if you have not moved or
looked around for two minutes, bond pauses until you do.

## Feeding for bond

Feed your bonded spirit **its taming item** and it gains 5% of the bond points
it already has, or 60 points if that is more. Each species takes only its own:

- Nightfox: sculk
- T-Rex: steak
- Baby Dragon: dragon's breath
- Mini Player: golden carrot
- Gremlin: cooked chicken
- Quokka: shrubs (short grass, ferns, tall grass or bushes)
- Direwolf: Golden Bone

It shows hearts when it works. It will only take one every ten minutes; the
bond screen shows how long is left next to the bond bar, and the spirit tells
you if you try too soon.

Ordinary companion food (golden apples, enchanted golden apples, cooked
chicken, cooked rabbit and sweet berries) gives a little experience, heals 2
hearts and adds 1 bond point. Cooked chicken is the Gremlin's taming item, so
a bonded Gremlin takes it as the bond feed above instead. A golden apple given
to a hurt spirit heals it, see [The Kindred Charm](./kindred_charm).

## Losing bond

Death is the only thing that lowers bond. When your bonded spirit dies its feed
cooldown restarts from ten minutes, and if its bond was above level 10 it drops
back to the start of the previous level. At bond 10 or below nothing is lost.

## What bond does

Every level of bond helps a little:

- **Faster revive.** The charm's five minute recovery shrinks as bond rises,
  down to half at bond 30.
- **More storage.** Slots unlock in step with bond, see [Storage](./storage).
- **Faster leveling.** Experience gains are multiplied by up to 1.5x at bond 30.

And three levels unlock something new:

- **Bond 5**: the spirit's special ability switches on. Each species has its
  own, listed on its page.
- **Bond 15**: every timed ability runs **twice as often**.
- **Bond 30**: timed abilities run **four times as often**, and you gain
  **+2 armour** whenever your spirit is within 16 blocks of you.

<Callout variant="info">
    Bond is kept when a spirit prestiges, so a spirit you have raised to bond 30
    stays there through every star it earns.
</Callout>
