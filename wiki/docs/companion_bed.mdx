A low wooden bed with a cushion, half a block tall, where a hurt spirit can
rest and heal.

## Resting

A tamed spirit walks to a bed on its own when all of these are true:

- it is hurt
- it is not fighting
- it is not on Stay
- you are within 16 blocks of it
- there is a free bed within 16 blocks of it

It settles on the cushion and gets **Regeneration II** for 10 seconds, renewed
every 10 seconds for as long as it stays there. It gets up once it is fully
healed, when it starts a fight, or when you walk more than 16 blocks away, so it
never gets left behind.

Each spirit takes its own bed. A bed another spirit is using or heading to is
skipped, so place one bed for each spirit you want resting at once.

Any spirit standing in a bed heals the same way, including one you have told to
Stay. Sit it in the bed and it stays there healing.

## Dyeing

Right-click the bed with any dye to recolour its cushion. The bed keeps its
colour when you break it and place it again.

## Crafting

Planks on either side of a hay bale, with a row of wooden slabs below. Any kind
of planks and slabs work.
