A tank. Slow, heavy and very hard to knock around, the T-Rex wades into melee
and pounces on anything that tries to keep its distance.

## Finding and taming

Brush suspicious sand in **desert pyramids** and **desert wells** and you will
eventually turn up a **T-Rex Egg**. Place it somewhere safe. It cracks twice and
hatches into a wild T-Rex after five minutes; a torch or campfire within two
blocks halves the wait, and lighting one part way through still helps. Breaking
the egg gives it back.

Tame it with **steak**, one in three chance per piece, and feed it steak later
to raise bond.

## Stats

30 health, 4 attack, 2 armour, 0.25 speed, 90% knockback resistance at level 1.
Growth 1.0x. Up to 18 storage slots. No special immunities.

## Combat

Melee. It runs whenever its target is more than 10 blocks away and drops back to
a walk once a leap is an option. It does not hunt dangerous prey.

## Abilities

- **Savage Leap** (level 1): when its target is 3 to 10 blocks away and it is
  on the ground, it pounces. Where it lands, its target and any other monsters
  within 2.5 blocks are hit and knocked back. The target takes 1.5x its attack
  damage and the other monsters take half that. 10 second cooldown.
- **Crushing Might** (level 1): Haste I while you are within 12 blocks.
- **Alpha** (level 1): monsters cannot spawn naturally near it. The radius is 4
  blocks at level 1 and grows by one block every three levels, to 14 at level
  30. Spawners are unaffected.
- **Alpha Boost** (bond 5): +15% attack damage.
- **Kindled Vigour** (level 10): heals itself half a heart every 5 seconds.
- **X-Ray Stomp** (level 30): every 60 seconds while you are within 8 blocks it
  stomps hard enough to shake your screen, and every ore within 12 blocks is
  outlined for you through the ground for 10 seconds.

Bond does not change how often its abilities run: Alpha, its base buff, has
no timer.

## Equipment

**Boxing Gloves** give it 3 more blocks of reach and 2 more attack damage. It
wears them, too: with the gloves on it swaps to its boxing look and its bite
becomes a punch.
