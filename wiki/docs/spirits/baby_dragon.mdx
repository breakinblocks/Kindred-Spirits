A glass cannon. It hovers rather than walks, hits harder than anything else at
level 1, grows faster than any other spirit, and goes down quickly if something
reaches it.

## Finding and taming

Place a **dragon egg** and let **lava** flow into a block next to it. The egg is
consumed and a wild Baby Dragon hatches. Lava cannot hurt it, so the pool that
hatched it is no danger.

Tame it with **dragon's breath**, one in three chance per bottle, and feed it
dragon's breath later to raise bond.

## Stats

10 health, 7 attack, 2 armour, 0.36 speed at level 1. Growth 1.5x. Up to 9
storage slots. Immune to fire and lava: it never burns, whatever it flies through.

## Combat

Ranged only. It keeps about 8 blocks away, backs off inside 4, and never lands a
physical hit; its damage is Dragon Breath. It will hunt dangerous prey on
Aggressive.

## Abilities

- **Dragon Breath** (level 1): every 3 seconds scorches its target within 10
  blocks for three quarters of its attack damage, sets it on fire for 4
  seconds and leaves a shrinking cloud of dragon's breath at its feet for 5
  seconds.
- **Forge Draft** (level 1): every lit furnace, smoker and blast furnace within
  4 blocks runs at **double speed**. The radius is 5 blocks from bond 15 and 6
  at bond 30.
- **Dragonfire** (bond 5): every hit it deals has a 25% chance to set the
  target burning for another 4 seconds.
- **Kiln Breath** (level 30): an ability you trigger yourself, from the button
  on the bond screen or `/kindredspirits smelt`, while within 16 blocks of it.
  Every item in your inventory that a furnace could smelt is replaced with its
  smelted result on the spot. 5 minute cooldown.

Bond does not change how often its other abilities run.

## Equipment

The **Dragon Tablet** swaps Dragon Breath for **Meteor Call**: every 4 seconds a
fireball drops from 12 blocks above its target (within 16 blocks) and deals 8
damage to every enemy within 2 blocks of where it hits, with a brief patch of
fire. While it carries the tablet it also gets restless: every 2 minutes there
is a one in four chance it wanders off for 30 seconds before going back to
whatever it was told. It really does carry it: with the tablet equipped it holds
the slate up in front of itself, screen glowing, and only swings it aside to
breathe.
