A small jungle companion with strong armour, gentle support abilities and the
largest storage capacity of the current spirits.

## Finding and taming

Find wild Quokkas in jungle biomes, in groups of one to three at ocelot rarity.
Tame one with **shrubs**: short grass, ferns, tall grass or bushes. Each feeding
has the normal one-in-three taming chance. Feed shrubs after bonding to raise bond.

When hurt by an attacker, a wild adult throws a tiny baby toward it and flees at
1.5x speed for ten seconds. These thrown babies cannot be tamed and disappear
after a minute. Naturally spawned babies can be tamed normally.

## Stats and storage

18 health, 2 attack, 6 armour and 0.36 speed at level 1. Growth is 0.5x.
Storage expands with bond to **27 slots**. It bites in melee and avoids dangerous
prey such as creepers when hunting aggressively.

## Abilities

- **Brighter Side**, from level 1: Luck I for you within 16 blocks,
  refreshed every five seconds.
- **Smile**, at bond 5: one random effect for you within 16 blocks for
  30 seconds: Regeneration I, Haste I, Speed I, Resistance I or Absorption I.
  Its cooldown is 120 minus three times the Quokka's level, in seconds, reaching
  30 seconds at level 30.
- **Always Happy**, at level 30: every two minutes, nearby compatible adult
  animals enter love mode if their species has at least two and fewer than
  sixteen animals within eight blocks. Breeding cooldowns still apply. Babies
  within eight blocks grow three times faster; this does not stack with other
  Quokkas and respects golden-dandelion age locks.

## Equipment: Quokka Snack

Craft a snack with **one shrub, wheat, an apple and a honey bottle**, in any
arrangement. Right-click your tamed Quokka with it to equip it.

The snack gives it a smiling face. While bonded and equipped, every thirty
seconds it has a 20% chance to charm a hostile mob within eight blocks. For
twenty seconds the charmed mob seeks the nearest other hostile within sixteen
blocks, leaves non-hostiles unharmed, and shows heart particles. Its ordinary
AI determines how it attacks. Charm wears off even if the Quokka leaves.

Disable individual abilities from the charm screen. The global abilities
setting also disables snack charm and all support effects.
