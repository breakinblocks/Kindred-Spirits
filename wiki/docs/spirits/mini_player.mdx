A small copy of a player. It wears a real skin, your own unless you say
otherwise, and fights the way you do: a sword up close, a bow at range.

## Finding and taming

Every **wandering trader** sells one Mini Player Spawn Egg for **32 emeralds**,
once per trader. Use the egg to place a wild Mini Player.

Tame it with a **golden carrot**, one in three chance each, and feed it golden
carrots later to raise bond.

## Stats

18 health, 4 attack, 2 armour, 0.30 speed at level 1. Growth 0.5x, tied with
the Quokka for the slowest. Up to 18 storage slots. No special immunities.

## Combat

Hybrid. Inside 3 blocks it draws a sword; past 6 it switches to a bow, and in
between it keeps whatever it was doing so it does not stutter. Arrow damage
comes from its own attack stat, not from the weapon.

The sword is a **copy of the weapon you are holding**, checked every second, so
it swings whatever you swing, enchantments included, or an iron sword if you
are unarmed. The copy never drops. Against dangerous prey it keeps the bow out
no matter how close they get.

Its arrows fly past you and your other spirits without hitting them.

## Skin

Open the bond screen and type a player name in the skin box to dress it as
that player. An empty box means your own skin. Servers can turn skin choice
off.

## Abilities

- **Mirror Strike** (level 1): whenever you hit something while it is within 16
  blocks of you and 12 of the target, it echoes a quarter of your blow onto the
  same target. Once a second.
- **Are You Gonna Eat That?** (level 1): whenever you finish eating, it heals
  for 10% of the food's nutrition, plus 1% per level, and you get a little extra
  saturation from the meal, 1% per level.
- **Helping Hand** (bond 5): a 5% chance that anything stackable you craft
  comes out doubled. The chance rises with bond like a timed ability.
- **Mending Presence** (level 12): heals you half a heart every 10 seconds
  while you are hurt and within 8 blocks.
- **Friendly Face** (level 30): Hero of the Village while you are within 16
  blocks, so villagers trade cheaper.

Bond does not change how often its abilities run: Are You Gonna Eat That?,
its base buff, has no timer.

## Equipment

Any **chestplate**. It wears it under the skin, gaining the armour, toughness
and every protection enchantment on it. The chestplate never wears out.
