Seven spirits can be found, tamed and bonded today. Each one fights differently,
grows at its own pace and helps you in its own way.

- [Nightfox](./nightfox): fast and evasive, fights from range with spirit flame.
  Lights up the dark for you. 1.0x growth, 9 storage slots.
- [T-Rex](./trex): a melee tank that pounces on its prey. Keeps monsters from
  spawning around you. 1.0x growth, 18 storage slots.
- [Baby Dragon](./baby_dragon): a glass cannon that hovers and breathes fire.
  Fans your furnaces. 1.5x growth, 9 storage slots.
- [Mini Player](./mini_player): a small copy of a player that swaps between
  sword and bow and wears a real skin. Shares your meals and your crafts. 0.5x
  growth, 18 storage slots.
- [Gremlin](./gremlin): fast, weak and chaotic. Pushes your machines along and
  eats your food. 1.5x growth, 9 storage slots.
- [Quokka](./quokka): a friendly jungle companion with Luck, animal care and monster
  charm. 0.5x growth, 27 storage slots.
- [Direwolf](./direwolf): a wolf one size up that digs things up for you and
  rallies you for a fight. 1.0x growth, 18 storage slots.

## Level 1 stats

- **Nightfox**: 35 health, 3 attack, 2 armour, 0.32 speed
- **T-Rex**: 75 health, 4 attack, 2 armour, 0.25 speed, 90% knockback resistance
- **Baby Dragon**: 25 health, 7 attack, 2 armour, 0.36 speed
- **Mini Player**: 45 health, 4 attack, 2 armour, 0.30 speed
- **Gremlin**: 25 health, 2 attack, 2 armour, 0.40 speed
- **Quokka**: 45 health, 2 attack, 6 armour, 0.36 speed
- **Direwolf**: 45 health, 5 attack, 4 armour, 0.32 speed, 20% knockback resistance

For scale, a wolf has 8 health and 4 attack at 0.30 speed.

## Dyeing

Right-click your own spirit with any dye to recolour its fur, hide or scales.
Eyes, teeth, claws, glowing markings and worn gear keep their own colours, and
even the darkest spirits take the full, bright colour. It keeps the colour
while resting in the charm. Right-click it with a water bucket to wash
the dye off; the bucket stays full. The Mini Player wears a player skin
instead, so dye does nothing on it.

## Skins

Some spirits have more than one look. When yours does, the charm screen shows
a Skin button under the name box; each click moves to the next skin and then
back to Default. The choice stays while the spirit rests in the charm, and dye
still works on top of it. The Mini Player picks a player skin by name instead.

## Dangerous prey

Creepers, ghasts, the Warden and the bosses are dangerous prey. The Nightfox,
Baby Dragon and Mini Player will hunt them on Aggressive because none of them
ever has to stand next to one; the Mini Player keeps its bow out against them
no matter how close they get. The T-Rex, Gremlin, Quokka and Direwolf leave them
alone, since walking a creeper back to you is not help.
