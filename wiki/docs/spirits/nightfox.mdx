A fox touched by the deep dark. Quick on its feet, it never bites: it fights
with spirit flame from a distance and keeps out of reach.

## Finding and taming

Right-click any fox with a block of **sculk**. The fox is gone and a wild
Nightfox stands in its place. Tame it with more sculk, one in three chance per
block, and feed it sculk later to raise bond.

## Stats

35 health, 3 attack, 2 armour, 0.32 speed at level 1. Growth 1.0x. Up to 9
storage slots. Immune to the Wither effect and to wither damage.

## Combat

Ranged only. It closes to about 8 blocks, backs off inside 4, strafes, and never
lands a physical hit; all of its damage is Shadow Ball. It will hunt dangerous
prey on Aggressive.

## Abilities

- **Shadow Ball** (level 1): every 3 seconds hurls a spirit flame at its target
  within 16 blocks for half its attack damage, and lifts the target off the
  ground for 2 seconds.
- **Swift Step** (level 1): Speed I while you are within 12 blocks. Speed II
  while it wears Running Shoes.
- **Wisplight** (level 1): every 10 seconds looks for a dark spot within 8
  blocks (block light 3 or less) and leaves a small glowing wisp there that
  gives off light 10. It keeps up to 16 wisps; each lasts 5 minutes or until the
  fox is more than 64 blocks away.
- **Night Light** (bond 5): the fox itself glows, lighting the block it stands
  on at level 12 wherever it goes.
- **Night Ward** (level 8): puts you out whenever you catch fire within 10
  blocks of it.
- **Kindled Vigour** (level 16): heals itself half a heart every 5 seconds.
- **One With The Night** (level 30): while you are within 16 blocks you have
  Night Vision, and Invisibility whenever you stand in block light 3 or less.

Wisplight runs twice as often from bond 15 and four times as often at bond 30.
Bond does not change how often its other abilities run.

## Equipment

**Running Shoes** make it 60% faster and raise Swift Step to Speed II. It
wears them, a boot on each paw.
