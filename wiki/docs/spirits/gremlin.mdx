A small, fast troublemaker. It is the worst fighter of any spirit and it grows
faster than almost anything, so what it is really for is the machinery around
your base: it pushes furnaces and other block entities along, steals your food,
and eventually starts growing redstone out of the walls.

## Finding and taming

Gremlins are made, not found. Feed a wild rabbit a **carrot** so it remembers
you, then feed that same rabbit a **cooked chicken** between midnight and dawn
(world time 18000 to 23000). The rabbit is gone and a wild Gremlin stands where
it was.

Only the player who fed it the carrot can turn it, and only in that window; at
any other time the chicken does nothing.

Tame it with **cooked chicken**, one in three chance per item, and feed it
cooked chicken later to raise bond.

## Stats

10 health, 2 attack, 2 armour, 0.40 speed at level 1. Growth 1.5x. Up to 9
storage slots. The fastest spirit on its feet and the weakest in a fight.
Immune to poison.

## Combat

Melee, and not good at it. It leaves dangerous prey alone even on Aggressive,
which is the right call for something with 10 health. Treat any fighting it does
as a bonus.

## Abilities

- **Tinker** (level 1): every 10 seconds, a `10 + level` percent chance to push
  one random ticking block entity within 4 blocks four ticks further along.
  Furnaces, brewing stands, hoppers, spawners, anything that ticks.
- **Snack Thief** (bond 5): every 3 minutes, a 50% chance to eat one food item
  out of your inventory, sparing whatever you are holding, and give you Strength
  I, Speed I or a quarter more height for a minute.
- **Swift Step** (level 12): quickens your pace while it is near.
- **Energized Chaos** (level 30): every 30 seconds, a 30% chance to charge one
  stone or deepslate block within 4 blocks into redstone ore. It never touches
  ore that is already there.

Tinker runs twice as often from bond 15 and four times as often at bond 30.
Bond does not change how often its other abilities run.

## Equipment

The **Battery** makes it a real tick accelerator, and it carries the battery
around in its hand. Every second there is a one in
ten chance it pushes a nearby block entity **eight** ticks further along, on top
of whatever Tinker is doing, and the jolt deals 2 lightning damage to every
hostile within 3 blocks of it.
