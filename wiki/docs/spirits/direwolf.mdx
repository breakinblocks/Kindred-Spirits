A wolf one size up: a balanced melee fighter that digs things up for you when it
is bored and leads the pack when the fight gets serious.

## Finding and taming

Craft a **Golden Bone** from a bone and eight gold nuggets, then use it on any
wolf. The wolf becomes a Direwolf where it stands.

- A **wild** wolf becomes a wild Direwolf. Tame it with more Golden Bones, one
  in three per bone, the same odds as a wolf and a plain bone.
- A wolf **you have tamed** becomes your tamed Direwolf straight away. It keeps
  its name, and if it was wearing wolf armour the Direwolf comes out wearing it.
- Someone else's wolf is left alone.

Feed Golden Bones after bonding to raise bond.

## Stats and storage

18 health, 5 attack, 4 armour and 0.32 speed at level 1, with 20% knockback
resistance. Growth is 1.0x. Storage expands with bond to **18 slots**. It
fights in melee and leaves creepers and other dangerous prey alone when hunting
on Aggressive.

## Abilities

- **Not Another Hole**, from level 1: whenever it has been standing around on
  soft ground (dirt, grass, sand or gravel) it digs for two seconds and drops
  something. Level 1 digs every 176 seconds, falling by four seconds a level to
  60 seconds at level 30, and twice or four times as often from bond 15 and 30.
  On dirt it mostly turns up bones, seeds and flint. On **sand** it rolls the
  desert pyramid, desert well and warm ocean ruin archaeology tables, and on
  **gravel** the cold ocean ruin and trail ruins tables, sherds and armour
  trims included. Walking off or getting into a fight stops the dig, and it
  tries again the next time it is idle.
- **Best Friend**, at bond 5: mobs killed by you or by the Direwolf while it is
  within 16 blocks of you drop half again as much experience.
- **Leader of the Pack**, at level 30: every 90 seconds while you are within 8
  blocks it howls, you get Resistance II for 20 seconds, and every hostile mob
  within 24 blocks glows for ten seconds. Only you see the glow.

## Equipment: Wolf Armour

Right-click your tamed Direwolf with **wolf armour**, dyed or not, to equip it.
The armour's 11 armour points apply exactly as they would on a wolf. It is
never drawn on the model and never loses durability.

Disable individual abilities from the charm screen.
