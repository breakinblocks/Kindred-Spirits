All commands sit under `/kindredspirits`.

## For everyone

- `/kindredspirits info` prints your bonded spirit's level, stars, experience,
  bond, saturation, rested experience, gear and abilities, with the requirement
  for any it has not learned.
- `/kindredspirits release` clears your bond, the same as the button on the
  bond screen.
- `/kindredspirits smelt` triggers Kiln Breath on a Baby Dragon that has
  learned it.
- `/kindredspirits skin <player>` dresses your bonded Mini Player in that
  player's skin, the same as the skin box on the bond screen. Leave the name off
  to go back to your own skin. It works while the Mini Player is resting too.

## For operators

- `/kindredspirits xp <amount>` grants experience to your bonded spirit.
- `/kindredspirits bond <points>` grants bond points to your bonded spirit.
- `/kindredspirits resetrevive <players>` clears the charm's recovery timer for
  those players.

## Keybind

**Cycle Companion Orders** (G by default, under the Kindred Spirits category in
the controls screen) cycles Follow, Stay and Wander on your bonded spirit.
