A spirit is a companion that travels with you: it fights beside you, carries
your things, grows stronger the longer you are together, and learns tricks that
help you out around your base. Getting one takes three steps.

## 1. Find a wild spirit

Spirits do not wander the world on their own. Each species has one way in:

- **T-Rex**: dig a T-Rex Egg out of suspicious sand and let it hatch.
- **Baby Dragon**: let lava flow against a placed dragon egg.
- **Mini Player**: every wandering trader sells one spawn egg for 32 emeralds.
- **Nightfox**: use a block of sculk on a fox.
- **Gremlin**: feed a rabbit a carrot, then cooked chicken after midnight.

Each [spirit page](./spirits/overview) has the details.

## 2. Tame it

A wild spirit only listens to its own taming item. Right-click it with that item;
each one is eaten and has a one in three chance of working, the same odds as a
wolf and a bone. Hearts mean it is yours.

- Nightfox: sculk
- T-Rex: steak
- Baby Dragon: dragon's breath
- Mini Player: golden carrot
- Gremlin: cooked chicken

A tamed spirit follows you and can be told to sit, but that is all. It has no
levels, no abilities and will not fight, and if it dies it is gone.

## 3. Bond with the charm

Craft a **Kindred Charm**, then sneak and right-click your tamed spirit with it.
The bond is what turns a pet into a companion: it starts gaining levels and
bond, its abilities switch on, it fights for you, and if it ever dies the charm
can bring it back.

You can only be bonded to one spirit at a time. The bond is yours, not the
charm's, so a lost charm costs nothing: craft another and it answers to the same
spirit.

<Callout variant="info">
    Right-click the charm on its own to open the bond screen. Everything you do
    with your spirit day to day happens there.
</Callout>
