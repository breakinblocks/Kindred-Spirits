Every spirit has one gear slot, and each species has its own kind of gear. Gear
changes how a spirit fights or moves; it is never drawn on the model.

- **T-Rex**: Boxing Gloves. Reaches three blocks further and hits for two more.
- **Baby Dragon**: Dragon Tablet. Its breath becomes a meteor, and it wanders now and then.
- **Mini Player**: any chestplate. The armour value and its enchantments apply.
- **Nightfox**: Running Shoes. 60% faster, and Swift Step gives you Speed II.
- **Gremlin**: Battery. Pushes nearby machines along and shocks anything hostile beside it.

- **Quokka**: Quokka Snack. A smiling face and a chance to charm nearby monsters into fighting each other.

The details are on each spirit's page.

## Putting it on and taking it off

**Right-click your tamed spirit with the gear.** If it was already wearing
something, the old piece comes back to you. A spirit only accepts gear made for
its species.

To take gear off, open the bond screen and press the small button on the gear
row. That works while the spirit is resting too.

## What happens to gear

- Sending a spirit to rest, or reviving it after a death, keeps its gear.
- Releasing the bond drops the gear at its feet, or hands it to you if the
  spirit was resting.
- An unbonded spirit drops its gear when it dies.

<Callout variant="info">
    A chestplate on a Mini Player never loses durability, so there is no reason
    not to give it your best.
</Callout>
