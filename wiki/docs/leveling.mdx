A bonded spirit levels from 1 to 30. Each level raises its base stats: health,
attack, armour and speed, with fast-growing species gaining more per level than
slow ones.

## Where experience comes from

Your spirit learns from you. Whenever you gain experience and your bonded spirit
is out and within 32 blocks, it receives **half of that amount** as its own. Kill
things, mine, smelt, breed animals: whatever earns you orbs earns it progress.
Feeding it also grants a little (4 points per meal).

Higher bond speeds this up, multiplying every gain by up to 1.5x at bond 30.

The first level costs 140 experience and each level after that costs 70 more
than the last, so the full climb to level 30 is about 32,000 points.

## Saturation

A spirit tires of the same fight. Every point it gains adds one to a saturation
counter that drains at one per second:

- Below 200 saturation it learns at full rate.
- From 200 it learns at **half** rate.
- From 500 it learns at a **tenth** of the rate.

Standing at a mob farm will run into this quickly. Saturation clears completely
when you move **three chunks** away from where it began, so the answer is simply
to go somewhere else for a while.

## Rested experience

Time away is not wasted. After five minutes without a gain, your spirit starts
banking rested experience at one point per second, up to 1,200, and it keeps
doing so while resting in the charm. While it has any banked, every gain is
**doubled**, with the bonus drawn from the bank until it runs dry.

## Stat growth

Each level adds a fixed amount to the level 1 stats, scaled by the species'
growth rate:

- Health: +1 per level
- Attack: +0.25 per level
- Armour: +0.1 per level
- Speed: +0.002 per level

A 1.5x species like the Baby Dragon gains half again as much of each; a 0.5x
species like the Mini Player gains half. Stars raise these further, see
[Stars and Prestige](./stars).

## Stat bonuses that are not levels

Some things raise stats on top of growth: stars, certain abilities (Alpha Boost)
and equipment (Boxing Gloves, Running Shoes). All of them show in the numbers
on the bond screen.
