Abilities are what make each species worth raising. Your **bonded** spirit
uses them wherever it goes, and any other spirit you have tamed uses them while
it is told to **Stay** (see Spirits on Stay below). The bond screen and the
charm tooltip list every ability of your species, green when learned and grey
with its requirement when not, and your spirit tells you in chat the moment it
learns one.

## How abilities unlock

Each ability has one requirement, either a **level** or a **bond level**. Every
species follows the same shape:

- **Level 1**: its attack, plus a base buff that is always working for you.
- **Bond 5**: a special that rewards time spent together.
- **Levels in between**: a few extras that vary by species.
- **Level 30**: its ultimate.

## Timed, reactive and active

Most abilities run on a timer while the spirit is out. Bond speeds up only the
**base buff**, the ability each species has from level 1: a timed base buff runs
**twice as often from bond 15** and **four times as often at bond 30**, and the
Baby Dragon's Forge Draft reaches one block further at each step instead. Specials
and ultimates keep their own pace.

Some react to something you do: landing a hit, crafting, eating. Those need the
spirit nearby, usually within 16 blocks.

One kind you trigger yourself. An **active** ability shows a button under the
portrait on the bond screen, labelled with the seconds left when it is cooling
down. Kiln Breath is the only one so far, and `/kindredspirits smelt` does the
same as the button.

## Switching abilities off

The Abilities button on the bond screen opens a list with an On / Off switch for
every ability you have unlocked. Off means off: the ability stops ticking,
stops reacting, no longer counts as your active ability, and any stat bonus it
was giving is removed. Wisplight and Night Light also clear the lights they
placed. The setting stays with the spirit through rest, revive and prestige, and
`/kindredspirits info` shows a switched-off ability as "(off)".

<Callout variant="info">
    Turn Wisplight off if you would rather your Nightfox did not decorate your
    base, or Alpha off if you are farming mobs near your T-Rex.
</Callout>

## Spirits on Stay

A tamed spirit you are not bonded to uses its abilities while it is on Stay, so
you can sit spirits around your base and let them work: a Baby Dragon by the
furnaces, a T-Rex keeping monsters from spawning, a Gremlin by your machines.
Sneak and right-click it with an empty hand to change its orders. It uses the
abilities it learned while it was bonded and does not level or gain bond, and it
still will not fight, so attacks such as Shadow Ball and Dragon Breath do
nothing. Abilities that buff you work when you come within their range.

**Right-click it with an empty hand** to open its ability list, with the same
On / Off switches as the bond screen, no charm needed. The list stays open while
you are within 8 blocks. Once it leaves Stay its abilities stop and it clears
any lights it placed.

When several spirits could answer the same reactive ability (crafting near a
Mini Player, eating near a Gremlin), only one does: your bonded spirit first,
otherwise the closest one on Stay.

## Range

Anything that buffs you needs you close, usually within 12 or 16 blocks. Your
spirit follows you anyway, so in practice this only matters when you leave it
on Stay.

<Callout variant="info">
    Abilities can be turned off entirely in the common config, which leaves
    spirits as decorative pets.
</Callout>

See each spirit's page for its full list with numbers.
