Your bonded spirit carries things for you. **Right-click it with an empty hand**
to open its pack.

## How it works

The pack belongs to you, not the spirit. There is one 27 slot inventory per
player, and your bonded spirit is only the way in. That means:

- Nothing drops when your spirit dies, and nothing is lost when it rests in the
  charm or you release it.
- Switching to a different spirit shows the same items.

## How many slots you get

Each species has a maximum, and slots unlock in step with your bond level up to
that cap. You always have at least 3 slots.

- Nightfox: up to 9 slots (one row)
- Baby Dragon: up to 9 slots (one row)
- Gremlin: up to 9 slots (one row)
- T-Rex: up to 18 slots (two rows)
- Mini Player: up to 18 slots (two rows)
- Direwolf: up to 18 slots (two rows)
- Quokka: up to 27 slots (three rows)

If you bond a new spirit or lose bond, slots you filled earlier may end up past
the current limit. They are shown darkened and are **remove only**: you can take
things out but not put things in until bond grows back to reach them. Nothing
is ever hidden.

The pack stays open only while the spirit is alive, bonded and within 8 blocks.
